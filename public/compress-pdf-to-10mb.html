Where a 10MB limit usually comes from

Job-ready documents sit in an awkward range: too heavy for most application forms, too light to justify a file-transfer service. These are the submissions that most often bounce at 10MB:

  • Applicant tracking systems. Resumes are small, but a portfolio or writing sample attached to the same application often is not.
  • Design and architecture roles. A dozen full-bleed spreads exported at print resolution will sail past the cap.
  • Learning management systems. Course platforms routinely set a per-file ceiling that students only discover at the deadline.
  • Online print shops. Uploaders accept PDFs but reject oversized ones before you reach the checkout page.

Every portal sets its own number, and they change. Check the upload page itself rather than trusting a figure you found elsewhere.

Keeping your portfolio readable at 10MB

  1. Export at 150 to 200 DPI. Screen viewing rarely needs more, and it is where most of the wasted weight sits.
  2. Flatten layered artwork before exporting. Layered files carry several copies of the same pixels.
  3. Crop mockup padding. Blank margin around device mockups is pure file size.
  4. Cut spare slides. Two extra process pages are often the difference between passing and failing the cap.

Compress PDF to 10MB: common questions

Is 10MB small enough for a design portfolio?

For most portfolios it is a practical middle ground. Image-heavy documents lose some detail as the file shrinks, so after compressing open the two or three spreads that sell your work and check them at full size before you submit.

Will compressing to 10MB flatten my layers or remove selectable text?

Page count, page size, and layout are preserved, and text normally stays selectable. When a file has very little room left, TinyPDF may fall back to rasterizing pages to reach the target, and rasterized pages are no longer searchable. The status message tells you when that happens.

My PDF is already 9MB. Can TinyPDF still shrink it?

Yes, as long as the target is smaller than the original. A file that is already below your portal's limit does not need compressing, and the tool will refuse a target larger than the file you uploaded.

How long do you keep my files, and do I need an account?

No account and no payment are required. Uploaded and compressed files are temporary and are automatically deleted after about one hour, so download your result soon after compression finishes.
