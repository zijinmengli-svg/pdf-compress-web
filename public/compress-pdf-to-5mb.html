Why 5MB is the size that actually gets through

Bigger limits exist, but they are shared across everything in the message. Shrinking one bulky file gives the rest room to breathe:

  • Email with several attachments. A single PDF can eat the whole allowance that a contract, an image folder, and a spreadsheet were supposed to share.
  • HR onboarding portals. New-hire paperwork often caps individual uploads well below intuition.
  • Insurance and claim forms. Supporting documents usually have a strict per-file ceiling.
  • Mobile sharing. Chat apps recompress aggressively on poor connections, and a lighter source file survives that second round better.

Attachment policies change and vary by domain, so treat 5MB as a safe target rather than a universal rule.

Before you reach for a smaller target

  1. Strip embedded attachments. Proposals frequently carry the source spreadsheet or video inside the PDF itself.
  2. Export internal drafts in grayscale. Nobody reviewing a draft needs colour, and the saving is immediate.
  3. Rescan at 200 DPI. Scanners default higher than most documents need.
  4. Split instead of shrinking. If every image matters, two files at their original quality beat one soft file.

Compress PDF to 5MB: common questions

Will my images get blurry at 5MB?

Text-heavy documents usually stay crisp, while photographs and full-page scans lose visible detail. Zoom to 100 percent on the most important image after compressing to judge whether the result is acceptable for your purpose.

My PDF is scanned. Why can't it reach 5MB?

Scanned pages are already compressed images, so there is little left to remove. Running OCR to convert pages into real text, or rescanning at a lower DPI, usually frees up far more space than recompressing alone.

Can I compress several PDFs at once?

TinyPDF handles one file per job. Run each file through separately and download it before starting the next one, since compressed files are deleted after about an hour.

How long do you keep my files, and do I need an account?

No account and no payment are required. Uploaded and compressed files are temporary and are automatically deleted after about one hour, so download your result soon after compression finishes.
