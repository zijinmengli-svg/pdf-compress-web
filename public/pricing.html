Pricing

TinyPDF compression is currently free. Paid checkout is not enabled yet. The following is the planned pricing model, not a charge for using the site today.

Planned one-time purchase

  • The first successful PDF compression result in a browser will be free to download, with no account required.
  • For a later successful result, the planned price is ¥9.90 in mainland China or a $1.99 USD base price elsewhere per result. There is no subscription or automatic renewal.
  • Payment will be offered only after a compressed result has been created. A failed compression will not open checkout or incur a charge.
  • The checkout will show the final amount, currency, applicable tax, and available payment methods before you confirm payment. Payment methods depend on your location and Paddle approval.
  • A purchased result will be available for download for about one hour after payment confirmation. Keep your own copy after downloading.

Some PDFs cannot reach a requested target size without unacceptable quality or document damage. Check the achieved size and result details before deciding whether to buy. See our Refund Policy for delivery problems and refund requests.
