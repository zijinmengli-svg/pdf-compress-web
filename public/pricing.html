Pricing

Each day includes one free successful compression. The allowance resets at 02:00 Beijing time (Asia/Shanghai) and does not accumulate. The site shows your available balance; purchasing requires email sign-in. If checkout is unavailable, no new purchase can be made.

Prepaid compression credits

  • Buy 1, 3, 5, or 10 credits at a time. Purchased credits are bound to your email account, persist across refreshes, and are not reset with the daily allowance.
  • The unit price is ¥9.90 in mainland China or a $1.99 USD base price elsewhere. There is no subscription or automatic renewal.
  • One successful compression consumes one credit, using the daily free allowance before purchased credits. A credit is temporarily reserved during processing; a failed compression or result outside the required target range releases it. Repeat downloads do not consume additional credits.
  • The checkout will show the final amount, currency, applicable tax, and available payment methods before you confirm payment. Payment methods depend on your location and Paddle approval.
  • Files are temporary and are deleted about one hour after processing. Download and keep your own copy before expiry; buying credits does not extend file retention.

Some PDFs cannot reach the requested size. An entirely unused purchase can be requested for a full refund to the original payment method; partially used packs require manual review, not an automatic full refund. See our Refund Policy.
