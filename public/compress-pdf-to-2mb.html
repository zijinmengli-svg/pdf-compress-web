Where a 2MB ceiling shows up

This is the size we most often hear about from people filling in forms that cannot be negotiated with:

  • Visa and consular uploads. Supporting-document sections frequently carry a hard per-file cap.
  • Government grant applications. Older portals were built when attachments were small, and the limits rarely move.
  • Bank and KYC verification. Identity document uploads are usually the strictest field on the form.
  • Insurance claims. Evidence bundles are assessed by a person reading them on screen, not by pixel peeping.

Sites update these numbers without notice. Always read the requirement on the form itself.

How to keep it legible at 2MB

  1. Check whether colour is required. Grayscale often halves the size with no loss of legibility.
  2. Scan at 150 to 200 DPI. Anything higher is wasted on a screen review.
  3. Drop the cover page. Decorative covers and instruction pages cost real megabytes.
  4. Split when it matters. Feeding two files into two fields keeps every page readable.

Compress PDF to 2MB: common questions

Will my scans still be readable at 2MB?

Text-heavy scans usually stay legible, while photographs soften noticeably. If the requirement is readability of a certificate or contract, compress one sample page first and inspect it at full size before running the whole file.

Why did my result stop above 2MB?

Some PDFs have limited room for compression. When the exact target cannot be reached safely, TinyPDF returns the smallest usable result instead of forcing the file lower and corrupting the pages.

Should I split my document instead of compressing it?

When legibility matters more than sending one file, splitting is the better answer. Two files at their original sharpness will read better than a single file squeezed to 2MB. TinyPDF processes one file per job.

How long do you keep my files, and do I need an account?

No account and no payment are required. Uploaded and compressed files are temporary and are automatically deleted after about one hour, so download your result soon after compression finishes.
