Refund Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

TinyPDF is currently free and is not yet accepting payments. This policy describes how we plan to handle a one-time paid PDF compression result if paid checkout is enabled.

When no payment is due

We will not request payment if compression fails or no usable result is created. The first successful result in a browser will be free under the planned pricing model.

When a refund is available

  • If the same purchase is charged twice, the duplicate charge will be refunded.
  • If payment succeeds but the paid result cannot be delivered, the payment will be fully refunded.
  • If a delivered PDF is corrupt or unusable, contact us. We will review the issue and arrange an appropriate refund where warranted.
  • Applicable consumer-law rights and Paddle's buyer refund policy remain available. Nothing here limits rights that cannot legally be waived.

How to request help

Email zijinnmengli@gmail.com with the purchase receipt or transaction reference, the approximate time of purchase, and a description of the issue. Please do not send a confidential PDF unless we request it. You may also use Paddle buyer support for payment or refund requests.

Payments and refunds, when enabled, will be handled through Paddle. TinyPDF will not ask you to receive an off-platform refund. See Paddle's Refund Policy for the payment provider's terms.
