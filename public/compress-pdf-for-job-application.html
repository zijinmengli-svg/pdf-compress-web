Compress a PDF for a Job Application

Prepare a resume, CV, cover letter, or short work sample for a portal that rejects large uploads.

Practical starting point: 5MB works for many application systems. Replace it with the exact limit shown beside the upload field when one is provided.

Submission check: compression can affect scanned certificates, headshots, charts, and linked text. Open the downloaded PDF and confirm names, dates, phone numbers, and URLs before sending it.

Choose the right document before compressing

Most resume PDFs are already small. Unexpectedly large files usually contain a high-resolution portrait, scanned signature, full-page background, or embedded work samples. Fixing that source problem preserves more quality than repeatedly compressing the same export.

  • Use a text-based PDF rather than a scan whenever possible.
  • Keep hyperlinks active and use standard fonts that embed cleanly.
  • Upload certificates separately if the form provides dedicated fields.

Final checks before clicking Submit

  1. Confirm the portal accepts PDF and note whether its limit is in MB.
  2. Search the downloaded file for your surname to verify that text remains selectable.
  3. Click the email, portfolio, and LinkedIn links inside the PDF.
  4. Check the first and last page so no export or upload step removed content.
