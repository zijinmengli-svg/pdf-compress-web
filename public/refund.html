Refund Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This policy covers prepaid packs of 1, 3, 5, or 10 compression credits purchased through Paddle. Purchasing requires email sign-in; purchased credits are bound to that account.

When no payment is due

One free successful compression is available daily, resetting at 02:00 Beijing time (Asia/Shanghai), without accumulation. The daily allowance is used before purchased credits. Failed compression or results outside the required target range release reserved credits. Repeat downloads do not consume additional credits.

When a refund is available

  • An entirely unused purchase may be requested for a full refund to the original payment method. A partially used pack requires manual review and is not eligible for automatic full refund.
  • Credits for a purchase under refund review may be frozen. An approved refund removes the associated credits; a rejected refund restores the frozen credits. Submission is not confirmation of approval or receipt of funds.
  • If a charge appears duplicated, credits do not arrive, or a delivered PDF is corrupt or unusable, contact us for investigation and an appropriate correction or refund. Do not pay again merely because delivery is delayed.
  • Applicable consumer-law rights and Paddle's buyer refund policy remain available. Nothing here limits rights that cannot legally be waived.

How to request help

Use Contact and provide the email used for purchase, approximate purchase time and issue description. Include the order number, receipt or Paddle transaction reference if available to help locate the payment. Please do not send confidential PDFs or payment-card details. You may also use Paddle buyer support.

Payments and refunds, when enabled, will be handled through Paddle. TinyPDF will not ask you to receive an off-platform refund. See Paddle's Refund Policy for the payment provider's terms.
