TinyPDF

FAQ

What does TinyPDF do?

TinyPDF compresses one PDF to a target size in MB. It is useful when a form, email system, portfolio site, or upload portal has a strict file-size limit.

Why did the result not reach my exact target size?

Some PDFs have limited room for compression. When an exact target is not safely reachable, TinyPDF returns the smallest usable result it can produce without intentionally damaging the file.

Will my page count or page size change?

The tool is designed to keep the same page count, page dimensions, and layout whenever possible.

Why might quality drop on very small targets?

If the target is much smaller than the original file, images may need stronger compression. In difficult cases, the tool may convert pages to images to reach a smaller size, which can reduce sharpness.

How long are files kept?

Uploaded and compressed files are temporary. They are automatically deleted after about one hour, so download your result soon after compression finishes.

Do I need an account?

No. TinyPDF does not require registration for the compression workflow.
