Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2, 2026

TinyPDF respects your privacy. This policy explains what information is processed when you use the PDF compression service. By using TinyPDF, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Process

We keep data collection limited to what is needed to run the service.

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Uploaded PDF files: Your file is used only to complete the compression job. Uploaded and compressed files are stored temporarily and are automatically deleted after about one hour.
  • Target size: The target size you enter in MB is used only for the current compression job.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Server logs: Standard request logs may include request time, IP address, request path, user agent, and status code. Logs are used for security, troubleshooting, and service reliability.
  • Analytics: We use Google Analytics 4 to understand traffic and feature usage. A first-party cookie named tinypdf_cid may be used to count visits more accurately. Analytics data is aggregated and does not include your PDF contents.
  • Advertising data: If ads are enabled, Google AdSense and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention.

3. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not require an account for the compression workflow.
  • We do not use your uploaded PDF files to train AI models.
  • We do not sell your uploaded files or file contents.
  • We do not intentionally inspect or reuse your file contents beyond what is needed to compress the PDF.

4. Security

  • Files are transferred over HTTPS when the site is served through HTTPS.
  • Temporary files are stored in server-side working directories used by the processing service.
  • Temporary files are automatically cleaned up after about one hour.
  • No online service can guarantee absolute security, but we use reasonable operational safeguards for this utility.

5. Third-Party Services

TinyPDF may use Google Analytics and Google AdSense. These providers may process data under their own privacy policies. You can learn more about Google's advertising data practices at Google Advertising Policies and manage personalized ads at Google Ad Settings.

6. Children's Privacy

TinyPDF is not directed to children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through this service, contact us so we can review the issue.

7. Your Choices

  • You can clear site cookies in your browser settings.
  • You can avoid uploading files that contain sensitive or confidential information.
  • You can contact us with privacy questions or deletion concerns related to temporary processing.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
